About

The bridge between engineering precision and operational delivery.

I'm a Program Management and Applications Manager at Sesotec India, with 7+ years of end-to-end ownership across conveyor and inspection system programs. I was promoted to Manager in April 2026 — having progressed through five internal roles since joining as a Technical Support Engineer in July 2019.

My work sits at the intersection of three disciplines: running complex multi-crore hardware programs, advising customers on whether and how our machines fit their production lines, and navigating India's regulatory frameworks — including independently securing AERB Type Approval for the Raycon EX2.

I hold a Post-Graduate Diploma in ML & AI from IIIT Bangalore and am actively working toward applying data-driven decision-making in operations leadership. Currently pursuing Six Sigma Green Belt (CSSC-accredited, lifetime validity).
